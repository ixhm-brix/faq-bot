FAQ Bot — widget demo

This page is a fake "organization website." The chat button you see in the bottom-right corner is the embeddable widget. It talks to the same bot you've been using on Telegram, with conversation memory keyed by your browser, not by your Telegram account.

To embed on a real site, the org just pastes this one line, anywhere on the page: 
<script src="http://127.0.0.1:8000/widget.js" async></script>

Replace 127.0.0.1:8000 with whatever domain you host the portal on.

Try asking it something your uploaded PDFs cover — for example:

  1. "What are your opening hours?"
  2. "Where are you located?"
  3. "Do you accept insurance?"
  4. Something that's not in your docs — to see the handoff behaviour.

The widget keeps a 12-hour conversation memory just like the Telegram bot, so follow-up questions like "what about Saturday?" still work.
